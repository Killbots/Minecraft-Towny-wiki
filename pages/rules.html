ModernMC Towny

Play fair. Build a good community.

A clear set of rules helps everyone enjoy the server.

Help keep ModernMC welcoming and fair. These rules apply to every player; Towny, Vanilla, and Creative have a few additional rules below.

General

  1. Do not bully, harass, or target other players.
  2. Do not advertise other servers or services.
  3. Do not spam or flood chat.
  4. Respect every player and staff member.
  5. Keep swearing and caps limited; this is a PG-13 server.
  6. Do not ask to become staff. Use the staff application process.

Towny

  1. Hacked clients and unfair mods are not allowed; schematics are an exception.
  2. Do not use AFK pools or other methods to bypass the AFK timer.
  3. Do not dupe items or exploit glitches.
  4. Do not forcefully remove a player from their town.
  5. Do not join a town to grief or raid it.
  6. Do not use teleport requests to trap or kill players.
  7. Do not grief or scam other players.

Vanilla

  1. Hacked clients and unfair mods are not allowed.
  2. Do not steal other players' items.
  3. Do not grief or scam other players.
  4. Do not dupe items or exploit glitches.

Creative

  1. Do not create inappropriate builds.

Need help? If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask staff before doing it.
