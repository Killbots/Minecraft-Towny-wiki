Fishing & Quests

Take a break from building and explore more ways to play.

ModernMC has PyroFishing and a custom progression quest called The Frontier Spirit. Use the in-game menus for current fishing discoveries and quest progress.

PyroFishing

Try /fish to open fishing help and menus. PyroFishing includes custom fish and bait; the codex explains what you have discovered and what to try next. Watch server chat for tournament announcements and follow the instructions shown when an event is running.

The Frontier Spirit Quest

ModernMC progression quest

The Frontier Spirit

Speak with Explorer Tom at Hub spawn to begin. The quest tracks blocks you break, so objectives count as you mine or chop; there is no separate item turn-in.

Progress: /quest opens the tracker. /frontier prints your objective in chat. Use /selector to travel to the Resource World.

Chapter I: The Frontier Spirit
  1. Speak with Explorer Tom at Hub spawn to start.
  2. Chop 16 Oak Logs.
  3. Use /selector to travel to the Resource World, then mine 32 Iron Ore.
  4. Return to Explorer Tom at Hub spawn to finish the chapter.

Reward: $1,500, 500 XP, and 8 Frontier Tokens. Wanderer is unlocked; claim the rank with /ranks.

Chapter II: The Adventurer's Trial (progress tracking incomplete)

The tracker lists 32 Copper Ore in the Resource World, followed by a return to Explorer Tom. The configured reward is $1,000, 750 XP, and 8 Adventurer Tokens. Adventurer is claimed through /ranks.

Progress note: The current quest script has no copper-ore tracking or turn-in handler. This objective may not advance; ask staff for help if you reach it.

Helpful fishing tips

  • Use the fishing codex to learn about unfamiliar catches and items.
  • Watch chat for tournament announcements and check the event instructions.
  • Ask in chat if you cannot find Explorer Tom, the Resource World, or a fishing menu.

Quest steps and rewards can change with server updates. Follow the tracker and current staff guidance if they differ from this walkthrough.
