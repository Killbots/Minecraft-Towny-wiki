Fishing & Quests

Take a break from building and explore more ways to play.

ModernMC adds PyroFishing and BeautyQuests alongside normal survival gameplay. Use the in-game menus and help to find current fish, bait, tournaments, and quest opportunities.

PyroFishing

Try /fish to open fishing help and menus. PyroFishing includes custom fish and bait; the codex and other in-game menus explain what you have discovered and what to try next. Watch server chat for tournament announcements and follow the instructions shown when an event is running.

Quests

Look for quest NPCs and quest menus around the server. BeautyQuests manages player quests, while Citizens provides the NPCs used by server features. Read each quest carefully so you know its objectives and any turn-in requirements.

Helpful tips

  • Keep quest items until you have completed or abandoned the quest that needs them.
  • Check quest progress in its menu before repeating an objective.
  • Use the fishing codex to learn about unfamiliar catches and items.
  • Ask in chat if you cannot find the right NPC or menu.

Quest availability, rewards, and fishing events are controlled by the server and can change. Use the current menu and announcements for exact details.
