Skills & AdvancedEnchantments

Build your character as you play.

ModernMC has two skill systems, AuraSkills and mcMMO. AdvancedEnchantments (AE) adds custom enchants and support items to gear. Each system works separately, so use the in-game menus to check your progress and the current AE options.

On this page AuraSkills mcMMO AE basics Enchantment categories AE item categories Finding items

AuraSkills

Open the skills interface with /skills. It shows your skills and progress. Gain experience by playing and following the activities listed for each skill in game.

mcMMO

Use /mcstats to view your levels and experience. Skill commands such as /mining and /herbalism show more detail.

/party opens mcMMO party options. An mcMMO party is separate from your Towny town.

AdvancedEnchantments (AE)

AE adds custom enchantments and support items for gear. An enchantment book usually shows its name, level, compatible gear, and any success chance or failure consequence.

Before using a book

Read its full lore. Check the compatible item, effect, level, and success or failure details before applying, trading, or selling it.

AE's enchant pool, rates, items, and menus are configured by the server. Use the current in-game AE menu and item descriptions to see what is available.

Enchantment categories

These categories group the enchants configured for ModernMC by what they do. The examples and rarity groups below come from the server's current AdvancedEnchantments configuration. The in-game book description remains the best source for exact effects and levels.

Offense

Attacks, extra damage, or effects that trigger when you hit a target.

Configured examples: Berserk, Lightning, Double Strike, Lifesteal, and Execute.

Defense & recovery

Damage reduction, recovery, or effects that trigger when you are attacked.

Configured examples: Ward, Hardened, Angelic, Arrow Deflect, and Overload.

Tools & durability

Effects that protect, repair, or improve equipment durability.

Configured examples: Restore, Abiding, and Reforged.

Movement & survival

Mobility, exploration, or protection from environmental hazards.

Configured examples: Jelly Legs, Anti Gravity, Springs, Aquatic, and Obsidianshield.

Utility & progression

Experience, soul collection, or effects that support other activities.

Configured examples: Inquisitive, SoulGrind, Soulbound, and Glowing.

Some enchants activate only after a specific action and may have a chance or cooldown. If a book will not apply, check gear compatibility and read the in-game failure message before trying again.

AE item categories

These are the items and systems listed in ModernMC's current AdvancedEnchantments configuration. Check the item lore and menu before using an item; rates and item results are set by the server.

Enchantment books

The Server Enchanter sells random books for experience:

  • Simple: 400 XP
  • Unique: 800 XP
  • Elite: 2,500 XP
  • Ultimate: 5,000 XP
  • Legendary: 25,000 XP
  • Heroic: 50,000 XP

Random Enchant Books are also enabled in random loot and villager trades. Use the menu to preview and purchase a group book.

Secret Dust, Magic Dust & Mystery Dust

Ultimate Secret Dust is the ULTIMATE-group version of Secret Dust. Right-click a Secret Dust pouch to reveal Magic Dust or Mystery Dust for its group. Magic Dust increases a matching book's success chance and the server setting also lowers its destroy chance. Mystery Dust is listed as a failed by-product.

Secret Dust is included in configured random loot and villager trades. Direct Mystery Dust chance is set to 0 in those two pools.

Scrolls for protection or changes

White Scroll: protects an item from destruction by a failed enchant book. The global failure settings currently leave the target item intact on failure.

Holy White Scroll: gives armor or a weapon a 100% chance to be kept on death. Its protection is removed after death.

Randomization Scroll: rerolls success and destroy rates on a book of the same group.

Remove or reorganize enchants

Black Scroll: removes a random enchant and turns it into a book. The configured book has 95% success and 5% destroy chance.

Transmog Scroll: reorganizes the enchantment lines on an item's lore.

Add enchantment slots

Slot Increasers add one slot for the matching group. Weapon, Armor, and Tool Orbs add slots to the corresponding gear type, up to the maximum shown on the orb.

Track activity

BlockTrak tracks blocks broken with a tool. FishTrak counts fish caught with a rod. MobTrak and StatTrak count mob and player kills with a weapon.

Track souls

Soul Tracker adds soul tracking to an item. Soul Gems are also included in the configured loot and trade pools. Soul enchants include Phoenix, Immortal, Divine Immolation, Paradox, and SoulGrind.

Rename items

Rename Tags rename eligible enchanted items. Color codes are enabled; formatting codes and words on the configured blocklist are restricted.

Custom armor sets & weapons

AE also defines these custom gear items:

Armor sets: Dimensional Traveller, KOTH, Phantom, Ranger, Supreme, Yeti, and Mother of Yijki.

Weapons: Time Splitter, KOTH Axe, KOTH PvP Sword, Phantom Scythe, and Supreme Fanny Pack.

The AE files define the items, but do not identify their player obtain sources. Check current server menus and announcements.

Finding and using AE items

ModernMC's AE configuration currently enables three routes:

  • Server Enchanter: buy books from the six experience-priced groups above. The menu also links to the Alchemist and Tinkerer.
  • Generated chest loot: enabled with a 5% chance for a container to receive AE items, up to two items per container.
  • Villager trades: enabled with a 3% chance of adding AE items. Trading all configured loot types is enabled.

The Alchemist combines two books of the same enchant and level into a higher-level book, or two Magic Dusts of the same rarity into dust with a higher success chance. The Tinkerer accepts books and offers the configured dust and experience trade. Follow the preview and confirmation in each menu.

  1. Inspect the item. Confirm its effect, compatible gear, level, and any success or failure details.
  2. Check your gear. Look at existing enchants and available capacity before applying a book.
  3. Follow the instructions. Use scrolls, dust, or other support items only as their lore describes.
  4. Ask if unsure. Check in-game help or ask staff before risking valuable gear.

Server settings can change. AuraSkills, mcMMO, and AE progress independently. Current menus, item lore, and staff guidance take precedence if an enchant, item, rate, or source changes.
