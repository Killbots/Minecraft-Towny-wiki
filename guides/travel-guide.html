Homes & Warps

Keep track of the places that matter.

HuskHomes lets you save personal destinations, and PlayerWarps lets players share public destinations. These are separate from Towny town spawns.

Personal homes

Use the home commands shown by the server's tab suggestions or /help to save a location and return to it later. HuskHomes manages personal homes and related teleport requests. If you cannot teleport, read the message shown in chat; safety restrictions or cooldowns may apply.

Player warps

Use /pwarp to open PlayerWarps and browse destinations other players have shared. Read a warp's description and check its location before travelling. Players who create a warp can manage it through the command or menu options shown in game.

Town spawns

As a town member, use /t spawn to teleport directly to your own town's spawn. Use /t spawn <town name> to travel to the named town's spawn, subject to Towny access settings. You can also use the long form /town spawn. A town spawn is managed by town leadership, not by HuskHomes or PlayerWarps.

Teleport availability can depend on where you are, combat or other server restrictions, and the destination's settings. The in-game response explains why a request is denied.
